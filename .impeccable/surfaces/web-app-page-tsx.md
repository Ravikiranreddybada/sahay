Scope: whole web app (web/app/page.tsx + components). Mode: Operate. Two areas: Patient (Chat, Health, WhatsApp) and Office copy (Traces, Evals, Knowledge & handoffs). Desktop-first; phone must not break.
Audience: Indian caregivers handling family health admin; reviewers/ops inspecting how answers are made. Equal weight.
Chosen direction: Prescription Pad & Rubber Stamp (roll assigned; user delegated the choice). Health tab borrows the pathology-report table grammar as a document inside the same clinic world.
Memorable moment: the verifier's violet stamp landing on an answer once it is checked.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every answer is written on the clinic's pad and stamped only when it is checked. Refuses the category default of white rounded cards, teal accent and chat bubbles.

OWN-WORLD: Neutral pad stock (#F4F4EF) and a whiter writing sheet, carbon-black typed ink, a maroon (#7A1F2B) printed letterhead band, Reynolds-blue ballpoint (#1F3FA6) for the member's own words, violet stamp-pad ink (#4E3D8F) for verifier marks, lab-report red for alarms. Flat ink, zero shadows; separation is ruled lines. Anek Latin + Anek Devanagari, Red Hat Mono for codes. Three text sizes plus one big number per view; rank by weight and case. Raises: current answer owns the sheet (vertical feed); rank by weight/case (timetable rack); flat unmodulated ink (guide map); one big number per view (type specimen).

STORY: The member asks, sees the steps tick off down the margin, reads a typed answer with stamped source codes, signs any write explicitly, and sees VERIFIED land. A reviewer flips to the office copy and finds the same turn as a trace.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Maroon letterhead band full width (Rx mark, Sahay / सहाय, area switch Patient | Office copy, signed-in patient). Below: patient register column left, the ruled pad sheet centre (answers as typed entries, no bubbles), "How this answer was made" margin right. Composer is the pad's last ruled line, send in maroon.
