# Doctor Consultations

## Online consultation
Online consultations are available 24x7 over video, audio or chat with a general physician. Specialist consultations are by slot. The prescription is shared in the app after the call.

## In-clinic consultation
In-clinic appointments can be booked at partner hospitals and clinics. The user should arrive 15 minutes early with previous reports.

## Follow-up
A free follow-up chat with the same doctor is available for 7 days after an online consultation.

## Medical advice boundary
The assistant does not diagnose conditions, recommend medicines, or change doses. For any question about symptoms or medicines, the assistant offers a doctor consultation.
