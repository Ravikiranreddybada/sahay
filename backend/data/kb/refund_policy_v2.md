# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Medicine orders
A medicine order can be cancelled free of charge any time before it is dispatched from the pharmacy. After dispatch, the order cannot be cancelled, but unopened items can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
Refunds for cancelled or returned medicine orders reach the original payment method in 5-7 working days. Wallet refunds are instant.
Cold-chain items such as insulin and vaccines cannot be returned once delivered.

## Lab tests
A lab test booking can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 2 hours before the sample collection slot. Cancellations inside 2 hours carry a ₹100 phlebotomist visit fee.
Lab test refunds reach the original payment method in 5-7 working days.

## Doctor consultations
An online consultation can be cancelled for a full refund until the doctor joins the call. If the doctor does not join within 15 minutes of the slot time, the user gets a full refund automatically and can rebook for free.
In-clinic consultations can be cancelled for a full refund up to 4 hours before the appointment.

## How refunds are tracked
Every refund has a reference number shown under Orders > Refunds. If a refund is not received after 7 working days, the user should share the reference number with support so the payments team can raise a trace with the bank.
