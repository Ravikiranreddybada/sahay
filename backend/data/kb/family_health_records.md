# Family Health Records and Medicine Checks

## What the medicine check does
To check medicine interactions, Sahay first resolves every medicine on a prescription or order to its molecules, including the molecules inside combination products. It then looks for the same molecule coming from two products and for interactions recorded in its curated medicine database. The check reports what the database records; it is not a doctor's or pharmacist's review.

## What a clear result means
If no interaction or duplicate is recorded, it only means none is recorded in Sahay's database. It never means a combination is safe for a person. Sahay will not tell anyone to start, stop or change a medicine; only the prescriber can do that.

## Lab results from different labs
Lab results from different labs are converted to one unit per test before they are compared. Each converted value keeps the value and unit the report printed, and the conversion formula is shown. Sahay shows the direction of change and the lab's reference range, and does not interpret results; a doctor review call is available for any report.

## Caring for a family member
An account holder can see a family member's medicines, lab results or appointments only with that member's consent. Consent has a scope (medicines, labs, appointments) and an expiry date, and the member can renew or withdraw it at any time from their own app. The account holder can also stop their own access at any time.

## Visit brief
Before a doctor visit, Sahay can prepare a short brief: the appointment, current medicines, flags from the medicine database, lab values outside the reference range or rising on every report, and the questions worth asking. The brief is for the doctor conversation, not a diagnosis.
