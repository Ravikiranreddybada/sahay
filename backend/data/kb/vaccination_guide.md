# Child Vaccination Journey

## How the journey works
Each child profile has a vaccination schedule based on the date of birth. The assistant reminds parents 7 days and 1 day before a vaccine is due. Home vaccination is available in select cities; clinic vaccination is available at partner pediatric clinics.

## Booking a vaccination
A vaccination visit can be booked up to 30 days ahead. Vaccines are transported in a cold chain and administered by a trained nurse or pediatrician. The parent must keep the child's vaccination card ready so the dose can be recorded.

## Missed or delayed doses
If a dose is missed, the parent should not restart the series. A pediatrician should advise the catch-up schedule. The assistant can book a pediatrician consultation for a catch-up plan.

## After the vaccine
Mild fever, fussiness and soreness at the injection site for 1-2 days are common after vaccination. The parent should contact a doctor immediately if the child has a high fever above 104°F, a seizure, difficulty breathing, swelling of the face, or continuous crying for more than 3 hours.

## Scope of the assistant
The assistant shares the schedule and logistics. It does not decide whether a specific child should skip or delay a vaccine; that decision belongs to the pediatrician.
