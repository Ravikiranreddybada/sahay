# Surgery Care Journey

## Journey stages
A planned surgery journey has six stages: consultation, diagnostics, cost estimate, insurance pre-authorisation, admission, and recovery follow-up. A dedicated care coordinator is assigned at the consultation stage.

## Cost estimate
The cost estimate lists room category, surgeon fees, anaesthesia, implants or lens, and consumables. Room category changes the final bill because many charges scale with the room rent.

## Before admission
The hospital shares pre-operative instructions. Common instructions are fasting for 6-8 hours before surgery, stopping certain medicines only on the surgeon's advice, and carrying ID, insurance e-card, and previous reports. The patient must never stop a prescribed medicine on their own.

## Day of discharge
Discharge usually takes 3-6 hours because of final billing and insurer settlement. The discharge summary lists medicines, wound care, and the follow-up date.

## Recovery follow-up
The care coordinator calls on day 1, day 3 and day 7 after discharge. Warning signs that need an immediate doctor visit: fever above 101°F, bleeding or pus from the wound, severe pain not controlled by prescribed medicine, or breathlessness.
