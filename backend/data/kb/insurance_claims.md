# Health Insurance Claims Guide

## Cashless claims
Cashless treatment is available only at network hospitals. The hospital raises a pre-authorisation request to the insurer before a planned admission, at least 48 hours in advance. For emergency admissions, the pre-authorisation must be raised within 24 hours of admission.
Pre-authorisation decisions are usually given within 4 hours during working hours. The final bill settlement at discharge typically takes 3-6 hours.

## Reimbursement claims
For treatment at a non-network hospital, the user pays first and files a reimbursement claim within 30 days of discharge.
Required documents:
- Discharge summary
- Final hospital bill with itemised break-up
- Payment receipts
- Doctor prescriptions and investigation reports
- Cancelled cheque or bank details for the payout
Reimbursement claims are settled within 30 days of receiving all documents, as per IRDAI guidelines.

## Claim statuses explained
- Submitted: the claim is received and awaiting review.
- Documents pending: the insurer needs more documents; the claim is on hold until they are uploaded.
- Under review: a medical officer is assessing the claim.
- Approved: the amount is approved and payout is scheduled.
- Partially approved: some line items such as consumables or non-medical items are not payable under the policy.
- Rejected: the claim is not payable; the rejection letter states the clause.

## E-card
The health e-card is the digital insurance card for the user and covered family members. It can be downloaded from the Insurance section and shown at the hospital insurance desk for cashless admission.
