# Lab Tests and Health Checkups

## Home sample collection
Home sample collection is available from 6 AM to 11 AM in serviceable pincodes. The phlebotomist arrives within the booked 1-hour slot and carries a sealed kit.

## Fasting instructions
Tests such as fasting blood sugar, lipid profile, and full-body checkups need 10-12 hours of fasting. Water is allowed during fasting. HbA1c and thyroid tests do not need fasting.

## Report turnaround
Most routine blood test reports are ready within 24 hours of sample collection. Culture tests can take 3-5 days. Reports are shared in the app and by email; abnormal values are highlighted and a free doctor review call is offered.

## Delayed reports
If a report is delayed beyond the promised time, the lab team is alerted automatically. The user can ask for an updated time; a re-collection is free if the sample is rejected by the lab.
