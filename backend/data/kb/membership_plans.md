# Care Plus Membership

## Benefits
Care Plus members get unlimited online general physician consultations, discounts on lab tests and medicines, and free home sample collection.

## Family cover
A Care Plus plan covers up to 4 family members including the primary member.

## ABHA health ID
Users can create or link their ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) ID in the profile section to keep health records in one place.
